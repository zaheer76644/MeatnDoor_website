Privacy Policy

Your privacy is important to us. Learn how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Welcome to Meatndoor!

At Meatndoor, accessible via our mobile applications and our Website: www.Meatndoor.com your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, disclose, and protect your information when you use our platform.

By using Meatndoor's mobile apps or website, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

a. Personal Information

  • Full name
  • Mobile number
  • Email address
  • Delivery address
  • Payment information (processed securely through third-party providers)

b. Device & Usage Information

  • IP address
  • Mobile device type and OS
  • App usage logs and browsing behavior
  • Crash reports and diagnostics

c. Location Data

  • With your permission, we may collect and use precise location data to improve order delivery and app experience.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and fulfill your orders
  • To personalize your experience
  • To provide customer support
  • To send you order updates and offers
  • To improve our app and services
  • To detect and prevent fraud or misuse

3. Sharing Your Information

  • Third-party logistics providers
  • Payment gateways
  • Analytics and marketing tools
  • Government authorities if required

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Security

We use commercially reasonable physical, electronic, and administrative safeguards to protect your personal information. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

5. Deactivating Your Account

You can request to deactivate or permanently delete your Meatndoor account at any time. Upon verification, your account and all associated personal data will be securely removed from our systems, except where retention is required by law.

To request deactivation, please request account deactivation using the following link https://meatndoor.com/deactivate.

For questions about this Privacy Policy or your personal data, contact us at:

Email: support@meatndoor.com
